Why this layout works

1
The type carries the page

At this size the headline reads as a poster, so the hero still says what you do before the 3D finishes loading.

2
Overlap creates depth

The device covers only the ends of the first two lines. Every word stays readable, and the layering makes the 3D look like it sits in the room rather than in a box.

3
The split gives the scroll a purpose

The headline moving out of the way is what makes room for the teardown, so the two motions read as one move.

4
Phones get a still device

On mobile the headline stacks above a pre-rendered device image. Same composition, no 3D cost.
